# Installing build54 for testing

Replace Cabrillo in its existing installation/data container, preserving saves.
Keep shortcut49 and your existing JIT provider setup. No new game ZIP is needed
for an existing supported import. The nine original FNA packages supported by53
remain covered; see `docs/CELESTE_INPUTS.md` and Play → How to get your game files.

Before starting Celeste, enable Settings → Cabrillo extras → Feather follows
finger. The choice defaults off and is remembered by this installation.
Use a fresh JIT-enabled process as usual. During feather flight, start a fresh
touch away from the action buttons and drag toward the direction you want to go.
The movement joystick is hidden during flight; its area also accepts targeting.
Lifting your finger supplies neutral input and the original slow coast. A faint
circle at Madeline marks the small neutral zone. The feather keeps moving:
this is steering, not dragging/teleporting the player or stopping midair.

Try turns, lifting/re-touching, pause/resume and the transition back to normal
movement. Jump, dash, grab and pause buttons remain separate. After normal Quit,
restart with the option off to compare the original joystick. If you already
use Motion Smoothing, also try your usual 60/120 setting. Export diagnostics into
this build's Results folder. Physical feel/display acceptance remains separate
from the automated host and simulator checks.
